# ADR 0003: Monthly Curriculum Plan QuestPDF Template Specification

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-10-04
- **Context:** PDF Document Compilation in BjjSM Modular Monolith (Curriculum & Planning Context)

---

## Context and Problem Statement
Instructors need a clean, print-ready, and digitally shareable PDF export of their published monthly teaching plans. The document must look professional on gym noticeboards, print cleanly on standard paper, provide digital interactive links / QR codes for technique videos, and strictly adhere to the project's Japanese martial arts brand identity.

## Decision Drivers
* Strict Brand Design System adherence: Brand Blue (`#20409A`), Brand Yellow (`#FFEE00`), Insignia Black (`#050505`), Crisp White (`#FFFFFF`).
* 60-30-10 UI rule: 60% white/light background, 30% structural black/blue, 10% yellow accent highlights.
* Print & digital dual usability: Legible typography for physical noticeboards, plus clickable hyperlinks and scannable QR codes for video drills.
* Technology: Fast, fluent, code-first PDF generation via **QuestPDF** (.NET) without headless browser overhead (e.g. Puppeteer/Playwright).

---

## Decision Outcome
Chosen Option: **QuestPDF Single-Page Landscape Document with Brand Design Tokens**.

### 1. Document Specifications
* **Page Format**: A4 Landscape (`PageSizes.A4.Landscape()`).
* **Margins**: 20pt uniform margins.
* **Colors (QuestPDF Hex Tokens)**:
  * `BrandBlue = Color.FromHex("#20409A")`
  * `BrandYellow = Color.FromHex("#FFEE00")`
  * `InsigniaBlack = Color.FromHex("#050505")`
  * `CrispWhite = Color.FromHex("#FFFFFF")`
  * `MutedSurface = Color.FromHex("#F8FAFC")`
  * `BorderGray = Color.FromHex("#E2E8F0")`
  * `MutedText = Color.FromHex("#64748B")`

### 2. Layout Structure

```
+-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
|  [Logo Emblem]   ACADEMY NAME - CURRICULUM SYLLABUS          OCTOBER 2026         |
|                  Program: Adults Fundamentals                Coach: Marcus Silva  |
+-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
|================================ BRAND YELLOW TRIM ================================|
|  OBJECTIVE: Master Guard Retention, Hip Framing, and Turtle Defense               |
+-------------------+-------------------+-------------------+-----------------------+
| WEEK 1            | WEEK 2            | WEEK 3            | WEEK 4 (or 5)         |
| Theme: Framing    | Theme: Retention  | Theme: Half Guard | Theme: Sparring & Rev |
|-------------------|-------------------|-------------------|-----------------------|
| [Tech 1] + QR/Url | [Tech 1] + QR/Url | [Tech 1] + QR/Url | [Tech 1] + QR/Url     |
| [Drill 2]         | [Drill 2]         | [Drill 2]         | [Drill 2]             |
| [Notes]           | [Notes]           | [Notes]           | [Notes]               |
+-------------------+-------------------+-------------------+-----------------------+
| Footer: Generated by BjjSM - For Academy Members Only - Page 1 of 1               |
+-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
```

#### A. Header
* Dark Brand Blue (`#20409A`) background container.
* Left: Academy emblem crest logo.
* Center: Academy Name, Program Name (e.g. "Adults Fundamentals"), and Published Month/Year in bold white text.
* Right: Head Instructor name and publication date.

#### B. Accent Divider & Monthly Objective Banner
* 4pt horizontal rule in Brand Yellow (`#FFEE00`).
* Light Slate (`#F8FAFC`) banner displaying the `MonthlyObjective` in Insignia Black with a small gold/yellow bookmark icon.

#### C. Weekly Grid (4 to 5 Columns)
* Equal-width columns arranged horizontally across the page.
* **Column Header**:
  * Top pill badge with Brand Blue background, Brand Yellow week number (`#FFEE00`), and bold white theme label.
* **Technique & Drill Entries**:
  * Tag chips indicating category (`Warmup`, `Technique`, `Drill`, `CoolDown`) with subtle color fills.
  * Technique title in bold Insignia Black.
  * Concise coaching notes in muted slate (`#64748B`).
  * If `ReferenceUrl` is present:
    * Clickable hyperlink formatted with an underline icon.
    * A compact (40x40pt) QR code rendered beside the technique for smartphone camera scanning when printed on paper.

#### D. Footer
* Minimalist footer with subtle gray divider line, generation timestamp, academy website, and pagination.

---

### 3. API Contract & Integration
* Web API Endpoint: `GET /api/v1/plans/{planId}/pdf`
* Response: `File(pdfBytes, "application/pdf", $"Syllabus_{programName}_{year}_{month:D2}.pdf")`
* Cache header: Cacheable for published plans with ETag based on `LastModifiedAt`.

---

## Consequences
* **Positive:** High performance, single-process in-memory PDF generation (tens of milliseconds with QuestPDF).
* **Positive:** Beautiful print aesthetic matching the martial arts brand and emblem.
* **Positive:** Seamless experience whether viewed digitally on mobile or printed on the academy notice board.
